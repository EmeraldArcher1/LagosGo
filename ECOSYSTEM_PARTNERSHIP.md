# LagosGo independence and separation notice

**Status:** Supersedes the previous ecosystem-partnership proposal.

LagosGo is a standalone product. It must not be presented as part of a group ecosystem, and must not promote or link to POSH9JA EXPORT & LOGISTICS, OJA, or any other separate business in its customer-facing experience.

Keep all repositories, deployments, databases, credentials, customer support, operations and release decisions separate. Do not share customer, driver, identity, trip, location, payment or incident data between businesses. No partner discovery cards, cross-promotional links, partner logos, shared sign-in or automatic integrations are approved for LagosGo.

LagosGo may independently develop its own mobility and delivery services under its own brand, subject to implementation, security, legal and operational release gates.

See [PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES.md](./PRODUCT_BOUNDARIES.md) for the binding product rules.
