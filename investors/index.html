INVESTOR OVERVIEW

Building Lagos’s market logistics layer.

LagosGo’s thesis is to connect traders, riders and customers through landmark-aware pickups, parcel delivery, market orders, batching and pickup points. The initial focus is validating the operating model corridor by corridor.

Read the LagosGo investment thesis: market-zone logistics, transparent pricing, batched delivery and an initial Lagos pilot.

Problem and approach

Lagos delivery is shaped by congestion, informal addressing, waiting time and fragmented market pickups. LagosGo is designed around market zones and practical handover points.

Potential revenue model

Potential streams include completed-delivery fees, business logistics, trader tools and pickup-point partnerships. Pricing and unit economics require pilot validation.

Pilot milestones

Proof points include active riders and traders, completed deliveries, delivery success, repeat orders, rider net earnings and contribution margin. No unverified traction figures are published.

Clear information. Practical next steps.

LagosGo is developing its pilot network. Service availability, pricing, eligibility and launch dates must be confirmed before making a booking or rider commitment.
