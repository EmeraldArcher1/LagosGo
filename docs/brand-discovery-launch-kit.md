# LagosGo discovery and profile launch kit

## Positioning
**LagosGo — Move people, parcels, and market orders across Lagos.**

LagosGo is developing a Lagos-focused network for rides, parcel pickups, market orders, scheduled delivery and consolidated drops. Its differentiation is a market-zone approach: stall-level pickup details, landmarks, pickup points and delivery workflows designed around Lagos trade.

## Google Business Profile setup checklist
1. First decide whether LagosGo has a customer-facing, staffed location eligible for a profile or operates as a service-area business. Do not publish a residential or unstaffed address merely to create a listing.
2. Use the actual business name consistently: **LagosGo**. Do not add keywords to the business name.
3. Select the most accurate primary category available; choose a delivery/transport category only if it accurately represents the service actually operating.
4. Use the verified website URL, a monitored phone number and real opening/contact hours.
5. Complete Google's verification process from the account authorised to manage the business.
6. Add real photographs and service descriptions only when they represent current operations. Do not imply a delivery service or app-store listing is live if it is not.
7. Ask real customers for honest reviews after completed service; never buy or fabricate reviews.
8. Keep the profile, website and LinkedIn details aligned and update service availability as the pilot changes.
