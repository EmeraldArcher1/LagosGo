# LagosGo Lagos Pilot — Public Campaign and Advert Production Brief

**Status: proposed campaign copy. Do not publish launch dates, discounts, live availability, government endorsements or driver-income promises until verified and approved.**

## Campaign idea

**LagosGo. Lagos moves better when we move together.**

A locally rooted mobility service designed around fair fares, safer trips, cleaner eligible vehicles and transparent driver earnings. The story must show that passengers, drivers, vehicle owners, local businesses and the city all matter.

## Driver and vehicle-owner recruitment version

**Your car. Your work. A clearer way to move.**

LagosGo is inviting Lagos-based drivers and vehicle owners to express interest in a new mobility pilot focused on transparent trip earnings, responsible vehicle standards and fairer passenger pricing.

You may register with your own eligible vehicle or express interest as a driver. Cars will be assessed for roadworthiness, valid documents and insurance, reliable condition, working safety equipment, air conditioning for the relevant category, and a clean, well-kept interior. Approved vehicles may retain their original colour during the pilot if they use the official identification system and meet all applicable rules.

No earnings are guaranteed. Driver/owner agreements, platform fees, vehicle requirements, payout rules and any incentives will be explained before participation.

**Register your interest. Help shape a mobility service built for Lagos.**

## Lagos State Government / Transport for Lagos meeting opener

LagosGo is developing a locally rooted mobility platform with a public-interest objective: safer and more accountable trips, transparent passenger pricing, sustainable driver earnings, and a pathway for qualified private vehicle owners and fleet operators to participate.

We would welcome a structured discussion with Lagos State Government and the relevant transport authorities about a limited, evidence-led pilot. We propose agreed vehicle standards, transparent fare experiments, privacy-conscious reporting, clear complaint escalation, and independent review of safety, service coverage and economic outcomes.

We are not presenting this as an existing government partnership or asking the State to endorse an untested service. We are seeking the right technical, regulatory and operational guidance to design a pilot that serves passengers, drivers, local businesses and Lagos.

## Investor pitch summary

LagosGo is a Lagos-first mobility platform designed to align four interests that are often in tension: passenger affordability, driver take-home earnings, fleet-owner returns and sustainable company economics.

The model will be tested with transparent fare components, capped and disclosed platform fees, auditable driver payouts, eligibility-based vehicle onboarding and traffic-protection experiments. The platform can grow from solo rides into airport transfers, deliveries, corporate mobility and fleet services, but each expansion depends on operational readiness and validated unit economics.

We are seeking strategic investment and potential pilot dialogue—not promising guaranteed returns or unverified government contracts. The investment case will be supported by a clear use of funds, downside/base/upside scenarios, pilot scorecard, safety gates and break-even assumptions.

## Creative direction

- Palette: LagosGo yellow and black, with white space and clear readable captions.
- Sound: contemporary Lagos rhythm, confident and warm; avoid using unlicensed music.
- Cast: a range of Lagos commuters, professional drivers, private vehicle owners and small-business operators; portray people respectfully.
- Visual proof: show fare transparency, vehicle checks, app-to-car identity matching and driver earnings breakdown only if those screens and processes exist in the live product. Otherwise label them as concept visuals.
- Accessibility: captions, high-contrast text, legible mobile-first layouts and a voice-over that makes sense without visuals.
- Avoid: claims that LagosGo is already live, guarantees the cheapest ride or highest driver pay, has 24/7 emergency response, offers insurance, has a government partnership, or has implemented features that are still planned.
- Before publishing: confirm pilot status, contact details, official registration URL, eligibility terms, legal review, music/image rights and approvals for any vehicle identifiers.

## Additional government proposal: transport-priority corridors

LagosGo also invites Lagos State to assess a limited public-transport priority corridor pilot. The proposal is to study clearly marked, properly authorised lanes for eligible public transport operators, with road-safety review, transparent access rules and enforcement. Existing BRT lanes remain subject to their current regulations unless the responsible authorities formally approve a change.

We are asking for a feasibility study—not claiming that LagosGo already has special lane access or that yellow road markings alone will solve congestion. The study should protect BRT service, test the effects on adjacent roads, account for capital and maintenance costs, and publish measurable results before any expansion.
