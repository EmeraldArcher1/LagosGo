    THE LAGOSGO STANDARD

    A better ride starts before pickup.

    Every vehicle and driver must meet a clear service standard before activation.

    ❄
    Working air conditioning

    Required for eligible car categories, with any exceptions disclosed before booking.

    ✦
    Clean inside and out

    Neat seats, clean windows, no persistent unpleasant odours, and a tidy exterior.

    ✓
    Safety and roadworthiness

    Seat belts, valid vehicle documents, tyres, lights and routine inspections.

    ☺
    Professional service

    Respectful conduct, punctual communication, no smoking during trips, and safe driving.

    Standards will be verified during onboarding and monitored through rider feedback and periodic checks. Specific legal, insurance and vehicle-age requirements must be confirmed before pilot activation.
