Investor overview

LagosGo

LagosGo is developing a market-to-door delivery and mobility network connecting traders, riders and customers through market zones, landmark-aware pickups, optional batch delivery and transparent pricing.

Explore the pilot productHome

Problem

Informal-market collections, poor addressing, traffic and costly single-stop trips make last-mile movement difficult for small businesses.

Product direction

Ride, parcel pickup, market delivery, scheduled services, pickup points and multi-stop routes in a locally adapted network.

Pilot approach

Start with a focused corridor, a verified trader cohort, rider supply and a small pickup-point network before expanding.

Traction and financials

No unverified user counts, revenue, delivery volumes, contracts or fundraising status are published. Investor materials should use dated, verifiable data.

LagosGo is in pilot-stage development. Availability, onboarding, safety, insurance, payment and service terms must be confirmed before launch. This page does not claim unverified traction or signed partnerships.
